# Code of conduct

Treat contributors with respect. Discuss code and design decisions with evidence
and specific feedback. Respect different levels of experience and the use of
Spanish or English. Get permission before sharing somebody else's messages,
identifying information or private files.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, unwanted sexual comments and disclosure
of private information are unacceptable in project discussions. If a
discussion becomes unsafe, stop engaging and contact a repository maintainer
through an established private channel. Share only the information needed for
review.

Maintainers can remove inappropriate content, pause a discussion or restrict
access. They should protect the reporter's privacy and explain decisions
directly to the people involved where practical.
